Hands-on cat rescue across the Bay Area

Izzy Mae Feline Rescue is a volunteer-run 501(c)(3) founded in 2024. We trap, neuter, and vaccinate community cats. We foster and adopt out the cats our local shelter can't take. We coordinate medical care for cats who would otherwise be euthanized. Every dollar goes to the cats — we have no paid staff.

What We Do

Our Programs

Hands-On Rescue & Humane Outdoor Cat Solutions

We are committed to humane, effective approaches for supporting outdoor cat populations:

  • Collaborating with government agencies and veterinary professionals to implement Trap-Neuter-Vaccinate-Return (TNVR) — a proven alternative to euthanasia for population control.
  • Educating the public and communities about humane solutions for outdoor cats.
  • Maintaining community feeding stations and shelters to provide consistent nutrition, a safe dry place to sleep, and emergency medical care when needed.
Kitten and Cat Rescue, Foster, and Adoption

We rescue cats and kittens in need, placing them into safe foster homes and ultimately into permanent, loving families.

  • All cats are spayed or neutered before adoption to prevent further overpopulation.
Partnerships & Advocacy

We work in partnership with other animal welfare organizations and government agencies to advocate for policies and programs that protect and support feline lives.
